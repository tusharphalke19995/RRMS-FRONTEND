Criminal Investigation Department (CID)

Branches:

  • Criminal Investigation Department
  • Economic Offences Wing
  • Cyber Crimes and Narcotics
  • CID-Forest Cell

Hierarchy:

Headed by Director General of Police, assisted by ADGP/IGP, DIGP and others.

Administrative Responsibilities:

  • Promotion of SDA/Typist
  • Preparation of Gradation List
  • Allotting Range/Unit for FDA/Stenographers

CID Wings & Divisions

INTERPOL Division

Supervised by ADGP/IGP, designated as INTERPOL Liaison Officer.

Technical Support Division

Supervised by ADGP/IGP, provides technical support to CID operations.

Administration Division
  • Motor Transport, Building & Estates
  • Petitions, NHRC/SHRC matters
  • Estate Officer & PR Coordination

Criminal Intelligence Units

Located across Karnataka - gather actionable intel and assist local police.

Homicide and Burglary Division

Investigates serious violent and property crimes, including custodial deaths.

Special Enquiries Division

Handles confidential inquiries and non-homicide IPC cases.

Economic Offences Wing
  • Economic Offences Division: Fraud, cheating, criminal breach of trust
  • Financial Intelligence Division: Coordinates with RBI, ED, DRI on banking/NBFC frauds
  • Deposit Fraud Investigation Division: KPIDFE & BUDS Act cases
  • Anti-Human Trafficking Unit: Human trafficking and crimes against women & children

Cyber Crime & Narcotics Wing
  • Cyber Crime Division: CCPS, cyber forensics, cyber awareness
  • Cyber Training & Research: I4C, JMIS, CCPWC, SIM blocking
  • Narcotic & Organized Crime Division: FICN, illicit arms, narcotics, piracy

CID Forest Cell

Supervised by IGP, this division investigates wildlife and forest offences.

  • 18 Forest Mobile Squads statewide
  • Coordinates with Forest Officers & Check Posts
  • Implements Forest Acts and Wildlife Protection Act
